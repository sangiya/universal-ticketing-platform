# TicketMesh — Consumer Manual (Web + App)

## 1. Getting started

TicketMesh lets you search, compare and book tickets and services from many providers in
one place. You can use it in a browser (Web) or install the mobile-responsive app (PWA)
from your browser for an app-like experience.

**Install the app**: in your mobile browser, open the TicketMesh site and choose "Add to
Home Screen" (install prompt) — it becomes an installable app.

## 2. Create an account / sign in

- Choose **Sign up**, enter your name, email and password. Confirm your email.
- Roles: you start as a **Customer**; agents/shops use the agent flow (see Agent manual).
- Sign in with your email and password from the app or web.

## 3. Browse the marketplace

- **Home** shows shops/agents and product categories near you.
- **Search** for tickets/service by location, dates and category; results come from every
  connected provider and are compared side by side.
- **Shop pages** render each shop's own theme (logo/colors) — white-label.

## 4. Book and pay

1. Pick a product and choose your seats/options.
2. Confirm the fare (currency shown for your tenant, e.g. LKR, USD).
3. Pay with your saved card, wallet or bank option.
4. You instantly get a ticket with a scannable code (QR/barcode).

## 5. My tickets

- See your upcoming and past bookings.
- **View/scan** your ticket from the ticket screen.
- **Cancel** (if allowed by the policy) and receive a refund per the cancellation policy.

## 6. Get support (24/7)

- From your app/web, open **Support**.
- Raise a ticket with subject, category and description.
- Track your ticket, chat with the support team, and get updates. Escalations are handled
  automatically if a ticket nears its SLA.

## 7. Safety & security

- Your account and payments are protected; TicketMesh runs fraud checks on transactions.
- If a transaction looks unusual, it is blocked automatically and flagged for review.
- Never share your password or verification codes.
